01 // Our Story

A neighborhood table, cooked with patience.

Ember & Oak began as a twelve-seat counter and a wood oven. More than a decade later, we still cook the same way: whole ingredients, open flames, and the quiet belief that good food takes whatever time it takes.

Every morning our kitchen receives heirloom vegetables harvested at dawn, ethically-caught seafood from Maharashtra's coastal fishermen, and micro-lot coffee beans roasted specifically for our wood-fired room.

04 // Meet the Chef & Master Roaster

“Fire is honest. It rewards patience and punishes shortcuts — I try to cook the same way.”

Marco Ruiz & Head Roastery Team

Executive Chef & Co-founder

Trained in San Sebastián and Lyon, Marco returned to build a dining room centered on an open wood-fired hearth and single-origin coffee roasting. Every dish and pour-over is designed around the alchemy of heat, smoke, and natural fermentation.
