## 🔒 Client Demo Anti-Theft & Inspect Protection

All 5 templates are equipped with **[`client-protection.js`](client-protection.js)** to protect your design intellectual property during client pitches:
- **Disables Right-Click (Context Menu)**: Prevents clicking "Inspect", "Save Image As", or copying elements.
- **Blocks Inspect & Source Shortcuts**: Disables `F12`, `Ctrl+Shift+I` / `Cmd+Option+I` (Inspect), `Ctrl+Shift+J` / `Cmd+Option+J` (Console), `Ctrl+Shift+C` / `Cmd+Option+C` (Inspect Element), `Ctrl+U` / `Cmd+Option+U` (View Source), and `Ctrl+S` (Save Page).
- **Anti-Copy & Drag Protection**: Text selection and image drag-and-drop are disabled across the page, while form inputs (name, phone, booking dates) remain 100% interactive.
- **DevTools Open Detection & Debugger Trap**: If developer tools are opened, a proprietary client preview watermark modal shields the screen and triggers a debugger pause until the inspector is closed.
- **Floating Toast Notification**: Shows a dark-glass badge warning (*"🔒 Source inspection is disabled on this preview"*) if a user attempts to inspect.
