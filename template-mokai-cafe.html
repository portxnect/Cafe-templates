Manual Brew Bar Methods

Calibrated daily to 1.35% – 1.45% Total Dissolved Solids.

01 //

Hario V60 Ceramic

High clarity, tea-like lightness that accentuates bright floral and acidic fruit notes.

• Ratio: 1:16 (15g to 240g)

• Water Temp: 93°C

• Brew Time: 2:45 min

02 //

AeroPress Inverted

Full immersion pressure extraction that highlights rich body and caramelized chocolate undertones.

• Ratio: 1:14 (18g to 250g)

• Water Temp: 88°C

• Brew Time: 1:30 min

03 //

Chemex Bonded

Triple-layered wood-collared filter removing all cafestol oils for the cleanest cup profile possible.

• Ratio: 1:15 (30g to 450g)

• Water Temp: 94°C

• Brew Time: 4:00 min

04 //

Slow Cold Drip

Gravity fed drip-by-drip through ice water over 12 hours. Zero bitterness, deep natural sweetness.

• 1 drip every 1.5 seconds

• Nitrogen chilled

• Served over hand-carved ice
